| # | Check | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scientific novelty positioning | **PASS.** The title and abstract state the question as empirical, not as an algorithmic-novelty claim; the Introduction explicitly lists the prior certificate study as a completed negative result and frames repeated critical-region rounds as the open question. The Discussion explicitly states "we do not claim that critical-region search is a superior targeting mechanism." No individually-established technique (critical paths, LNS, portfolios, selective repair) is claimed as new; `02_prior_work_and_novelty.md` documents this per-component. |

| 4 | Experimental fairness | **PASS.** Every external LNS control is explicitly matched to the candidate on initial incumbent, round policy, local window, global continuation and worker count; size-matching is verified by test (`test_size_matching_of_controls`) and reported as exact in the development doc.
